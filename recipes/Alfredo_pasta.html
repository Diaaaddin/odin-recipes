Chicken Alfredo Pasta

Description

Chicken Alfredo Pasta is a rich, creamy Italian-American dish featuring tender pan-seared chicken breast over fettuccine tossed in a buttery garlic, heavy cream, and Parmesan cheese sauce.

Ingredients

The Pasta and Chicken

  • 8 ounces fettuccine pasta
  • 2 large chicken breasts (sliced into strips)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

The Alfredo Sauce

  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 1/2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup Parmesan cheese (freshly grated)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon fresh parsley (chopped, for garnish)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Cook the Pasta
    • Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
    • Add fettuccine and cook according to package instructions.
    • Drain the pasta and set it aside.
  2. Sear the Chicken
    • Season chicken strips with garlic powder, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
    • Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
    • Add chicken and cook for 5 to 6 minutes until golden brown and cooked through.
    • Remove chicken from the skillet and set aside.
  3. Make the Alfredo Sauce
    • In the same skillet, melt the butter over medium heat.
    • Add minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
    • Pour in the heavy cream and bring to a simmer.
    • Let it simmer for 5 minutes until it thickens slightly.
  4. Combine Everything
    • Turn off the heat and stir in the grated Parmesan cheese until completely melted.
    • Add the cooked pasta and chicken back into the skillet.
    • Toss everything together until the pasta is well coated in sauce.
    • Season with extra salt and pepper if needed.
  5. Garnish and Serve
    • Top with fresh chopped parsley.
    • Serve immediately while hot.
