butter chicken

Description

butter chicken or murgh makhani is a curry made from chicken cooked in a spiced tomato and butter-based gravy. The gravy is known for its creamy, rich texture. It is similar to chicken tikka masala, which uses a tomato paste.

Ingredients

Butter chicken ingredients include chicken thighs, plain yogurt, and garam masala

The Chicken Marinade

  • 1.5 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs (cut into bite-sized pieces)
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt (preferably Greek yogurt)
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon minced ginger
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt

The Sauce

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil (or ghee)
  • 1 large onion (chopped)
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon minced ginger
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 can (14 ounces) tomato puree or crushed tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 1/4 cups heavy cream
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Step-by-Step Instructions

1. Marinate the Chicken

  • Combine all marinade ingredients in a bowl with the chicken.
  • Cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.

2. Sear the Chicken

  • Heat oil or ghee in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  • Add chicken pieces in batches to avoid crowding.
  • Cook for 3 to 4 minutes on each side until browned.
  • Remove chicken from the skillet and set aside.

3. Build the Sauce Base

  • Add onion, garlic, and ginger to the same skillet.
  • Cook for 5 to 6 minutes until onions are soft.
  • Stir in cumin, garam masala, coriander, and chili powder.
  • Cook the spices for 1 minute until fragrant.

4. Simmer the Sauce

  • Pour in the tomato puree and let simmer for 10 minutes.
  • Pour the sauce into a blender and blend until smooth.
  • Return the smooth sauce back to the skillet.

5. Finish and Serve

  • Stir the heavy cream, sugar, and butter into the sauce.
  • Add the cooked chicken and any juices back to the pan.
  • Simmer for another 8 to 10 minutes until chicken is cooked through.
  • Season with extra salt if needed.
